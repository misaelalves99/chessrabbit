Use this page when sharing ChessRabbit on GitHub, Reddit, LinkedIn, Lichess forums, TalkChess, Product Hunt or a personal portfolio.

## One-line pitch

ChessRabbit is a free Windows chess analysis app with Stockfish, Leela support, PGN review, studies and local game storage.

## Short post

I built ChessRabbit, a free desktop chess analysis and training app for Windows.

It installs with a normal `setup.exe`, runs on your computer, stores games locally and supports Stockfish, Leela Chess Zero and custom UCI engines. It includes PGN import, game review, studies, opening work and training tools, with no paid plans or license keys.

Download:
https://github.com/shivamjg101/chessrabbit/releases/latest

Repo:
https://github.com/shivamjg101/chessrabbit

Feedback, bug reports and feature ideas are welcome.
